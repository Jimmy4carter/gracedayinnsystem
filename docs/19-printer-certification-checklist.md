# Front Desk Printer Certification Checklist

Record hotel, date, operator, terminal code, OS/browser versions, printer make/model/driver/firmware and paper width. Repeat for every supported combination.

## Required cases

- 80 mm and any approved 58 mm profile: page width, margins, no clipping or blank trailing page.
- Long guest name, long receipt/invoice/payment references and maximum monetary values.
- Cash, card/bank transfer labels; first print and visibly numbered reprint.
- Cover open, paper out, printer offline and browser print cancellation: job becomes failed with reason, then retries and records attempt count/completer.
- A4 fallback prints line totals, subtotal, tax, paid and balance.
- Guest can view only their receipt; staff access and result commands are audited.
- Ten consecutive receipts preserve numbering and do not double-post payment/cash movement.

## Acceptance evidence

- Attach photographs/scans for first print, reprint, long-content and A4 fallback.
- Record measured paper result, failed/retry job references and any driver settings.
- Front-desk lead and finance owner sign. Any failed combination remains unsupported until retested.
